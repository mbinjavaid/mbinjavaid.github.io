I’m a PhD candidate at RWTH Aachen, advised by Martin Grohe in the Logic and Theory of Discrete Systems group.

My research applies machine learning and reinforcement learning to molecular discovery, process design, catalyst development, and the circular economy. I also work on molecular and polymer property prediction for materials design, and on LLM-based extraction of structured data from the scientific literature.

If our interests overlap, please get in touch.
